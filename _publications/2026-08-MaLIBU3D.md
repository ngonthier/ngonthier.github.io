Abstract
======

MALiBU3D is a large-scale multitask ALS benchmark: 59 billion LiDAR points over 2,221 km² of metropolitan France. It combines and processes existing open-access data released by public authorities: the point clouds come from the French national LiDAR HD programme, and the remaining data are derived from IGN products — BD ORTHO, BD TOPO, Masque Forêt and FLAIR-HUB — and from the national CarHab habitat map. It spans urban, agricultural, forested, mountainous, and coastal landscapes, with point-wise intensity and aerial RGB.
